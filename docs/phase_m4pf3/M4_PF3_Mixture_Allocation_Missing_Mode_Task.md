# M4-PF3 Mixture Allocation and Missing-Mode Repair Task

## Objective

Starting from the frozen PF2-C result, determine whether the remaining
proposal inefficiency is primarily caused by probability-mass starvation of
existing mixture components, missing support in variance-dominant regions, or
neither mechanism.

## Required order

1. Verify the live PF2 tag, artifacts, hashes and full regression.
2. Freeze PF3-0 theory, sources and routing thresholds before diagnostics.
3. Reuse the 24 PF2 S0-anchor gradient archives with zero new simulator calls.
4. Diagnose allocation mismatch and tilted support coverage.
5. Generate birth candidates geometrically but admit them only through the
   M2 directional-derivative firewall.
6. Freeze exactly one route: allocation, coverage, mixed, or neither.
7. Only for an authorized intervention route, preregister fresh construction
   at the PF2 P11 anchor before new data.
8. Evaluate the locked candidate family with the same absolute FreeOracle VRF
   gates used by PF1 and PF2.

## Invariants

- Frozen state set: 24 M3-BV2 Value-Axis states, 12 WIDEN and 12 SHRINK.
- PF3-0 simulator calls: zero.
- Diagnostic anchor: persisted PF2 S0-selected scalar anchors.
- Intervention anchor: PF2 P11; S0 gradients cannot be reused as P11 gradients.
- No covariance-rank increase, mean-step sweep, controller tuning, M3-Q,
  descriptive covariance fitting, or post-hoc route changes.
- Confirmation definitions, seeds, costs and gates must be committed before
  confirmatory data.
- Primary endpoint: median statewise FreeOracle VRF with a selected-arm
  deployable cost of 100,000 samples.

## Verdict boundary

PF3 may establish feasibility only for the frozen benchmark, current mixture
parameterization, locked diagnostic/candidate rules and FreeOracle accounting.
It cannot establish universal missing modes, unseen-event generalization,
wall-clock speedup or deployability.
